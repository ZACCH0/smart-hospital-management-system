FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?+

Create an account, log in, choose your doctor and a time slot. Reception confirms your booking and you get notified.

Can I see my medical records online?+

Yes. Patients can view visit history, prescriptions and lab results from their dashboard at any time.

Is my health data safe?+

SHMS uses role-based access, scoped records and audit logging. Doctors see only their own patients, and every key action is logged.

How do I pay my bills?+

Invoices are generated automatically per visit. You can review itemised bills and track payment status from the billing section.

Can I try it before registering?+

Yes — the live demo lets you explore every role with one click and sample data. No real patient records involved.
