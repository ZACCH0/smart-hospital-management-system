What SHMS Offers

Designed from the ground up to solve clinical workflow bottlenecks and enhance hospital efficiency with real-time digital coordination.

Scheduling

Appointment Booking

Patients can book appointments with doctors. Receptionists can manage and approve schedules seamlessly.

EHR Records

Electronic Medical Records

Doctors can record diagnoses, symptoms, treatments, and prescriptions digitally with complete history logs.

Financials

Billing & Payments

Generate invoices automatically and track payment statuses for every single patient hospital visit.

Pharmacy

Pharmacy Management

Track medicine inventory, monitor stock levels, and flag expiring medicines automatically.

Diagnostics

Laboratory System

Doctors can request lab tests, and results with file attachments can be uploaded directly online.

Insights

Analytics Dashboard
