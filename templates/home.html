What SHMS Offers

📅

Appointment Booking

Patients can book appointments with doctors. Receptionists can manage and approve schedules.

🏥

Electronic Medical Records

Doctors can record diagnoses, symptoms, treatments, and prescriptions digitally.

💰

Billing & Payments

Generate invoices automatically and track payment status for every patient visit.

💊

Pharmacy Management

Track medicine inventory, monitor stock levels, and flag expired medicines automatically.

🧪

Laboratory System

Doctors can request lab tests, and results with file attachments can be uploaded directly.

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Admins get real-time charts showing appointments, revenue, and hospital statistics.
